# DJ B.A.E. Out-of-Town Opportunity Scout: Monday, Oct 5, 2026 (read-only)

**Rules followed:** I only looked at public pages. I contacted no one, submitted nothing, and changed nothing in GitHub or Supabase.
**Sources:** pages I opened directly are marked **(fetched)**. Pages I only saw as a search snippet are marked **(search result)**.
**Labels:** VERIFIED CURRENT SIGNAL means a call is open now and the deadline or date is visible. RELATIONSHIP CLUE means there's a booking pattern or a future call, but nothing open now.
**Fit basis** (from public pages): open format, hip-hop and Chicago house roots, queer (they/she). Prior cities are Chicago, Atlanta and Boston.

## Access limits
- **X/Twitter:** I couldn't read it. Search needs a login, and the public mirrors are dead or suspended, so nothing here comes from X.
- **Instagram/Facebook:** mostly behind a login. I relied on festival sites, ticketing pages (Eventbrite, Posh, Mato, HappeningNext) and press.
- **Original music:** many showcase calls (SXSW, The Great Escape, NXNE) are built for artists who release their own music. I found no public evidence of original releases by DJ B.A.E., so treat that as a fit risk for those calls.
- **Visas and work permits:** for Canada, the UK and the EU, playing paid sets usually involves work-permit or visa rules. I didn't verify the specific rules. Check official immigration guidance before accepting anything.

---

## Bottom line
Only three calls are paid and fit reasonably well. Two are open now and one opens Nov 1:
- **SXSW 2027** showcase: deadline Nov 20. Costs $80 to apply and pays $175 to a solo US act.
- **NXNE 2027** in Toronto: deadline Dec 31. Free to apply and pays $100 to a solo act.
- **Pride Toronto 2027**: opens Nov 1, with published DJ rates of $200–$500.

None of the US Pride calls I checked are open today. Atlanta Pride opens in March 2027, and the others are "closed" or "join the list." The strongest leads for getting DJ B.A.E.'s name out are the relationship clues: Chicago queer house parties (Queen! at Smartbar, Party Noire), Black house series (My Soul To Keep in Houston, Black Hause in Dallas and Atlanta), and Atlanta's femme-DJ Haus Party.

## Noise to skip next time
- **Pay-to-play and touring "talent calls":** Mexican Rave "Join The Rave" (21 cities, lumps DJs in with influencers, pay not stated, touring brand); A3C artist submissions via Sonicbids (paid platform; selected acts get a conference pass, not pay); paid pitch platforms such as Groover (the ADE 2026 Discovery call through Groover closed Sep 6).
- **Unpaid calls:** BlerDCon 2027 (badge only); open decks in DC (CGT at Sinners & Saints, Thurst Lounge) and London (Queer Open Decks at Dalston Superstore). Fine for networking, but not paid gigs.
- **Wrong city or wrong country:** Black Planet's "DJ jobs" page is a Swedish collective paying in kronor, not DC. An Eventeny "Entertainer Application" labeled as SF Pride was actually St Pete Pride (FL, closed).
- **Residency-locked calls:** Chicago Park District Night Out in the Parks (Chicago residents only); Future Front Texas DJ call (Texas artists).
- **Genre or format mismatches on aggregators:** Cheltenham Jazz "...around town" (needs a jazz connection; due Oct 23); Tallinn Music Week (Oct 26) and FOCUS Wales (Nov 1), which are original-music showcases; Sauna Fest North Island in NZ (Jan 15, very far); MUTEK (no DJ mixes).
- **Stale or unpaid pages:** Stonewall Columbus (mostly volunteer); Pride organizations' 2025–2026 forms that still rank high in search.
- **Search-engine summaries:** these mislabeled cities, called closed calls "open," and confused orgs. Always open the source page.
- **Excluded categories:** Green Line Talent (Detroit corporate DJ staffing). Any other DJ staffing companies, agencies, wedding-DJ companies or house/resident jobs that came up were skipped.
